About & Contact

About me

I started in automotive engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, where I led a team that built a four-wheel-drive, four-wheel-steer electric vehicle. That project led me into embedded software, and I went on to write firmware and test tooling at Bosch and FPT Software.

I am now an M.S. student in Software Engineering at Kennesaw State University, working as a graduate research assistant on fault localization and LiDAR point-cloud detection.

I enjoy the hardware-facing side of software: bringing up boards, debugging communication buses and building the test tooling that makes firmware dependable.

Interests

  • Cyber-physical and automotive systems
  • Real-time communication networks
  • Embedded firmware and hardware bring-up
  • Test automation and debugging
